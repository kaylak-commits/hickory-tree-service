## Which Type of Land Clearing Do You Actually Need?

Three jobs get called "land clearing" and they price and run differently. **Full
clearing for a new build** takes every tree, stump and root mass off the buildable
footprint, graded to the pad the builder specifies. **Partial clearing**, opening a
view, a driveway path or a pasture edge, leaves specific trees standing and only clears
a defined area, priced smaller than a full-acre job. **Brush and overgrowth clearing** covers land that has grown up over several years, saplings, vines and underbrush rather than mature trees. It is usually the fastest and least expensive of the three, since a mulching head can process it in place. If you are not sure which describes your
project, we walk the property with you before pricing anything.

## How Long Land Clearing Takes in Hickory

A single acre of light brush and overgrowth can be cleared in a day with a mulching
head. A heavily wooded acre with mature trees takes longer, since larger trees get
felled and processed individually rather than mulched in place, and stump removal adds
its own time on top of the clearing itself. Site access matters too, a lot that
equipment can reach directly moves faster than one needing a path cut in first. You get
a time estimate after the walk-through, based on your actual acreage and what is on it.

## How a Land Clearing Job Runs in Hickory, Start to Finish

Every land clearing job follows the same four stages, whether it is a quarter acre of
brush or several wooded acres for a new build.

### Walk-Through and a Written Price
We walk the property to see what is actually there, mark any trees that stay, and
confirm access for equipment. You get a price per acre before anything is cleared.

### Marking and Protecting What Stays
Trees, fence lines, septic fields and anything else staying in place get flagged before
equipment moves in, so nothing gets taken out by mistake.

### The Clearing Itself
Brush and smaller growth across the acre get mulched in place with an excavator-mounted head. Larger
trees are felled individually, then processed separately rather than pushed with the
brush.

### Hauling, Chipping and Grading
Larger trunks and unusable material get hauled off, brush gets chipped or left as mulch
per your preference, and the site is left graded and walkable for whatever comes next.

## What Goes Wrong When Land Clearing Is Done Badly in Hickory

Pushing everything, brush and mature trees alike, into a single pile rather than
processing separately wastes usable timber and leaves a burn pile that takes months to
break down. Clearing without marking property lines or a septic field first can put
equipment where it should not be, an expensive mistake on land that looked empty.
Leaving stumps and root masses in a footprint meant for a building pad causes settling
problems later, once soil gets placed over ground that was not actually clear. Clearing
in wet conditions without accounting for drainage can leave standing water on graded
land that used to shed rain through the trees that are now gone.

## What Land Clearing Includes in Hickory (and What It Doesn't)

We clear brush, remove trees of any size, and grind or pull stumps as the job calls
for. We do not do the grading, fill or utility work a build site needs after clearing,
that is a different trade, and we can point you toward who does it. We do not clear
land inside a wetland or stream buffer without confirming that with you first, since
that work can carry its own permitting separate from tree removal. If a lot has a
mix of healthy timber worth saving and growth that needs to come out, we say so at the
walk-through rather than clearing it all the same way.
