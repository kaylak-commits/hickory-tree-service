## When Should You Actually Get a Tree Health Assessment?

Four situations call for one before anything else. **A tree looking stressed but not
obviously dying**, thinning canopy, unusual leaf color, slower growth than trees
nearby, is the clearest case, since the cause could be disease, pests, soil or root
damage. **Before a tree is clearly failing**, catching a problem early gives treatment
or soil work a real chance, where waiting for obvious decline often narrows the option
to removal. **Before or after storm damage**, an assessment tells you whether a tree
that lost limbs or shifted at the base is still structurally sound. **Before buying or
selling a property with mature trees**, a written assessment documents condition for a
transaction the way a home inspection documents the house.

## How a Tree Health Assessment Runs in Hickory, Start to Finish

### Site Walk and Species ID
The arborist identifies each tree in question, loblolly pine, oak, maple or otherwise,
since the disease and pest entities that matter differ by species.

### Structural and Root Checks
Beyond species-specific disease, the arborist checks for decay pockets in the trunk,
water sprouts (thin upright shoots that often signal canopy stress or old improper
pruning), and root plate movement or other signs of poor root stability, any of which
can mean a tree fails structurally even while it still looks healthy from the ground.
Canopy dieback, dead sections scattered through an otherwise living crown, gets traced
back to a cause rather than treated as one problem.

### Checking Against Known Entities
Every tree's findings get checked against the species-specific issues that actually
occur here, southern pine beetle, oak wilt, verticillium wilt and the rest, rather than
a generic health check.

### The Recommendation
You get a plain next step for the tree: monitor, treat, fertilize, cable or remove,
with the reasoning behind it stated in the visit.

### The Written Record
A report documenting the tree's condition and our recommendation goes to you the same
visit or shortly after, usable for your own records, an insurer or a buyer.

## What Goes Wrong Without a Real Assessment in Hickory

Skipping the assessment and guessing at a removal call can take down a tree that soil
work or targeted treatment could have saved. Early southern pine beetle and
verticillium wilt cases often look worse than they are from the ground. The opposite mistake happens just as often. A tree with an already-fatal infestation, brown
needles working down from the top and white pitch tubes on a loblolly pine, gets left
standing on the hope it recovers. Once southern pine beetle is confirmed, standard
guidance calls for prompt removal to protect nearby trees. Treating the wrong problem, feeding a tree with
an active disease entity instead of addressing the disease, wastes money and time while
the real issue keeps spreading.

## What We Check, Species by Species

Southern pine beetle in loblolly pine shows as needles browning top-down with white
"popcorn" pitch tubes on the bark, fatal within weeks once established. Oak wilt in
white and red oak shows as rapid crown wilting and patchy leaf drop, spreading to
nearby oaks through root grafts and beetles if not addressed. Verticillium wilt in red
maple causes sudden branch death and is soil-borne rather than airborne. Water oak can
be hollow inside while looking healthy from the outside, which is part of why a visual
check alone is not a diagnosis.

## What Tree Health Assessments Include in Hickory (and What They Don't)

We diagnose tree health against known species and disease entities, assess structural
