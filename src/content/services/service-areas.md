Tree Service Hickory NC covers Hickory and the rest of Catawba County, part of the
Hickory-Lenoir-Morganton metro area of roughly 377,000 people. Removal, trimming, stump grinding, land clearing, hazardous tree removal, deadwood and diseased tree removal, and emergency storm response are all priced the same way. It does not matter which town on this list the call comes from. No separate travel charge applies
anywhere in the service area.

## What Determines Whether We Cover Your Address

Distance from Hickory rarely decides whether a job is a fit, equipment access does. A crew and
truck can reach most residential and small commercial properties across the county
without issue. A very rural lot with a long, narrow, or unimproved driveway may need
a quick phone check before scheduling, mainly for jobs requiring a crane or a larger
mulching machine for land clearing.

## Why We Cover the Whole County, Not Just Hickory

Catawba County runs about 170,000 people across Hickory and the smaller towns around
it, and tree work does not stop at a city line. A homeowner in Newton has the same sandy loam and the same storm exposure as one
inside Hickory city limits. A rural lot near Maiden often has more trees on it, not
fewer, than a smaller in-town yard. A lakefront lot near Sherrills Ford or Terrell carries
its own shoreline root and wind exposure that an inland yard never sees.
Covering the full county rather than just the city means one call handles the job
regardless of which side of a town line the property sits on. See our
[Catawba County](/catawba-county) page for the full list of towns and their local
detail.
