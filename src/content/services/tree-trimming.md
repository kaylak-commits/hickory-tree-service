## Why Hickory Trees Need Regular Trimming

Hickory gets about 45 inches of rain a year over roughly 107 days, and the sandy loam
soil under most yards here holds a tree's roots less firmly than denser soil once the
ground saturates. A heavy, untrimmed canopy catches more wind and more rain weight than
a thinned one. A well-pruned tree can shrug off a storm that drops a limb from a
crowded, top-heavy one. Catawba County also sees close to 85 freezing days a year, and
each freeze-thaw cycle widens any weak branch union that has not been cut back.
Loblolly pine, common across this area, tends to hold dense, top-heavy growth that
catches wind hard when it has not been thinned. Water oak and red maple carry a different risk, a codominant trunk with included bark
at the union. Structural pruning while the tree is young can often correct that weak
union before it becomes a removal problem.

## Which Type of Trimming Do You Actually Need?

Four jobs get called "tree trimming" and they are not the same work. **Structural
pruning** on a young tree fixes a weak union or a competing leader while a single cut
can still correct it. Wait too long, and that same defect turns into a removal problem. **Canopy thinning** on a mature tree removes selected interior branches to let
wind pass through instead of catching the whole canopy, without changing the tree's
shape. **Deadwooding** removes dead or dying branches only, usually the fastest and
least expensive of the four. **Clearance pruning** cuts back limbs that have grown into
a roofline, a power line or a neighboring structure. A tree with a split trunk or a
hollow section needs more than pruning. That is a removal candidate, and the inspection
will say so rather than pruning around a problem that will not go away.

## How Long Tree Trimming Takes

A single tree with deadwooding or light clearance pruning usually takes under two
hours. Canopy thinning on a large, mature tree takes longer, since every cut is
selected individually rather than made in bulk. A crew working from a bucket truck can
move faster than one that has to climb. Multiple trees on one property add time in
proportion to their number and size rather than a flat rate per tree. You get a time
estimate at the inspection based on the actual trees on your property.

## How a Tree Trimming Job Runs, Start to Finish

Every trimming job follows the same four stages, from a single ornamental to a mature

### Inspection and a Written Price
We look at each tree's structure, which limbs are dead, weak or growing where they

### Protecting Your Property
Anything under the drop zone, a fence, a garden bed, a parked car, gets covered or
moved before the first cut. On jobs near a roofline, limbs get lowered by rope rather
than dropped.

### The Pruning Itself
Cuts are made just outside the branch collar, the swollen area where a limb joins the
trunk, so the tree seals the wound properly instead of leaving it to rot. Structural
cuts, thinning cuts and deadwood removal are made in that order.

### Cleanup and Haul Away
Every limb and branch gets chipped or hauled off the same day. The yard is cleared of
debris before the crew leaves.

## What Goes Wrong When Tree Trimming Is Done Badly

**Topping** cuts main limbs down to stubs instead of pruning to a lateral branch. It is
the most common bad practice in this trade, and it is illegal in Hickory wherever the
city's landscape code applies. Topped limbs also grow back weak and fast, more likely
to fail in the next storm than the limb they replaced, the opposite of what trimming is
supposed to accomplish. A flush cut is the other common mistake, one made too close to
the trunk, past the branch collar. That removes tissue the tree needs to seal its own
wound, which invites rot into the trunk itself. Over-thinning a canopy in one visit, rather than spreading
the work over a couple of seasons, can shock a tree. That shock triggers a flush of
weak regrowth exactly where a storm will catch it hardest next time.

## Standards We Cut To

Pruning cuts follow ANSI A300 standard practice, the industry standard for where and
how a cut is made relative to the branch collar and the trunk. We do not top trees,
regardless of what a homeowner requests, and we will explain the alternative, usually
canopy thinning or clearance pruning, that gets the same result without the damage.
Loblolly pine and other heavy-canopied species get thinned rather than topped for wind
resistance. Oak and maple structural work is timed to the tree's dormant season where
the job allows it, which reduces stress on the tree.
