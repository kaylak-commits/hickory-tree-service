## Why Conover Sees the Same Tree Issues as Hickory

Conover sits close enough to Hickory that the ground underneath both is the same
sandy loam, which drains fast but still saturates during a heavy storm and holds a
tree's roots less firmly than denser soil. That is part of why a mature loblolly pine
or water oak in a Conover yard uproots rather than just drops a limb. See our
[Tree Removal](/tree-removal) page for the full local climate detail.

## How a Conover Removal Runs

The same four-stage crew process applies here as anywhere else in the service area. We
inspect the tree's lean, size and distance from any structure or power line and give
you a price in writing. Drop zones get marked and anything nearby, a fence, a garden
bed, a driveway, gets protected before the first cut. Open-yard trees come down whole, and anything close to a house or the road comes
down in sections instead, lowered by rope rather than dropped. Limbs and trunk sections get hauled off the same day, and the
stump can be ground in the same visit if you want it gone.
