## Why We Cover the Whole County, Not Just the City

A tree does not check the map before it falls, and the county's own geography makes that
true from one end to the other. The same sandy loam and roughly 45 inches of rain a year
sit under Hickory, Newton and every town in between. That combination is why mature trees
uproot rather than just lose limbs across the entire county, not only inside the city
limits. A
homeowner in Startown deals with the same species and the same storm systems as one on a
tighter lot in Conover, just with more acreage around the trees involved.

## What Determines Whether We Cover Your Address

Distance from Hickory rarely decides whether a job is a fit, equipment access does. A
crew and truck reach most residential and small commercial properties across all 170,000
residents of this county without issue. A rural lot with a long, narrow or unimproved driveway is common in places like Startown
or Maiden. That kind of lot may need a quick phone check before scheduling, mainly for a
job needing a crane or a larger mulching machine.
