## Lakefront Properties Near Lake Norman

Lake proximity changes what a removal job looks like in Sherrills Ford more than it does
closer to Hickory. A tree growing right along the shoreline often has roots that spread
shallow and wide to reach the water table, instead of deep and anchored. That shape makes
it more prone to a full uproot in wind than the same species set back from the bank. Many lots
here also carry a dock or a shoreline view a homeowner wants preserved, so a removal has to
account for what equipment can reach without crossing someone else's property line. The
community sits at an elevation just under 900 feet, lower than much of the county further
inland, which keeps the ground near the water table wetter for a larger part of the year.
