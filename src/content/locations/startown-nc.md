## Startown's Rural, Larger Lots

Startown spans close to 19,000 acres of mostly residential and farm zoning, and county
planning records show most of that land sits vacant or in tracts of four acres and larger,
rather than tight subdivision lots. That kind of acreage is exactly what makes land
clearing a bigger part of the work here than it is closer to Hickory's city limits.
Floodplain along the Henry Fork and Jacob Fork rivers also means some lots carry standing
timber that has never been thinned, so a clearing job can involve more trees per acre than
a typical yard. See our [Land Clearing](/land-clearing) page for the full local detail.

## What Land Clearing Costs in Startown

Clearing an acre of wooded or brushy ground in Startown runs $2,500 to $12,000 or more,
depending on vegetation density, stump removal and how easily equipment can reach the
site. Full tree removal on an individual tree still runs $500 to $3,000 with a $500 minimum

## How a Startown Clearing Job Runs

A crew walks the property first to confirm boundaries, mark any trees you want left
standing, and check for floodplain or wetland areas that change how equipment can move.
Brush and undergrowth clear first, then standing timber comes down, and stumps grind or
pull depending on what the finished site needs. Debris hauls off or mulches on site,
whichever fits the job.
