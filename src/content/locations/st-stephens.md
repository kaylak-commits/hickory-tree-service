## A Large Community Along Lake Hickory

Saint Stephens covers close to 10 square miles and carries more residents than most of
Catawba County's incorporated towns combined, even without its own town government. Homes
near the Lake Hickory shoreline sit on ground that stays wetter longer than a lot further
inland, since the water table follows the Catawba River closely along that edge. A canopy
left untrimmed near the water catches more wind off the lake than the same tree would
inland. Regular pruning matters more here for that reason than it does in a sheltered
yard further from the shoreline. See our [Tree Trimming](/tree-trimming) page for the
full local detail.

## How a Saint Stephens Trimming Job Runs

The same four stage crew process applies here as anywhere else in our service area. A crew inspects each
tree's structure and confirms a price before any cut is made. Anything under the drop
zone, a dock walkway, a garden bed, a parked car, gets covered or moved first. Cuts are
made just outside the branch collar so the wound seals properly, and every limb gets
chipped or hauled off before the crew leaves the property.
