## Claremont's Established Neighborhoods

Claremont's median household income sits above $66,000, a figure that tracks with a small
town built mostly of long-owned single family lots rather than newer subdivisions. Trees
planted when those homes went up decades ago are now the mature oaks and maples that split
limbs, drop large deadwood or lean toward a roofline as they age. The town also sits on the same sandy loam as the rest of Catawba
County, soil that drains fast but still saturates during a heavy storm and holds a root

The same four stage crew process applies here as anywhere else in our service area. A crew inspects the
tree's lean, height and distance from any structure, then gives a price in writing before
the first cut. Drop zones get marked, and anything nearby, a fence, a flower bed, a
driveway, gets protected first. An open yard tree comes down whole, and one standing close
