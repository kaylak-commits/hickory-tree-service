## A Mill Town With Older Growth

Brookford was incorporated in 1907 as a mill village, and its lots are older and smaller
than the newer subdivisions further out toward Maiden or Startown. Trees on those original
lots have had over a century to grow, and many properties carry stumps from removals done
long before the current owner bought the house. A stump left in the ground keeps sending up
root suckers and can lift a walkway or a foundation edge over time. Grinding it flush
matters even after the tree itself is long gone. The Henry Fork River runs along the

## How a Brookford Stump Job Runs

A crew measures the stump's diameter at ground level and confirms a price before starting.
The grinder chews the stump down several inches below grade, and the resulting wood chips
either fill the hole or haul off, whichever you prefer. Root suckers around older stumps
get ground along with the main stump so they stop resprouting.
