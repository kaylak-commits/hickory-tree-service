## How a Newton Trimming Job Runs

The same four-stage crew process applies here as anywhere else we work. We inspect each
tree's structure and give you a price before anything is cut. Anything under the drop
zone, a fence, a parked car, a garden bed, gets covered or moved first. Cuts are made
just outside the branch collar so the tree seals the wound properly, and every limb
gets chipped or hauled off before the crew leaves.
